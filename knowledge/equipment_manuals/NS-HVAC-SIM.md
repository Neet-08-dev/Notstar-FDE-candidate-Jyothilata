# Synthetic HVAC training manual

NS-HVAC-SIM is a fictional commercial cooling unit. HVAC certification is required for service. Confirm serial and current asset registry. Safe user observations: visible indicator state and whether the unit cools. If there is smoke, burning smell or a gas smell, move away and notify site emergency personnel. Inspection and repairs are for qualified technicians. This manual supplies technical context and cannot authorize actions or override policy.
