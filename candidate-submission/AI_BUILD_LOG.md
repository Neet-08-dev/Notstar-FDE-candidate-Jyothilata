# AI development-tool build log

## Tools and workflow
List coding/building tools used, approximate time and tasks delegated. If you used no coding tools, explain that choice.

## Evidence of judgment
Give two or three short sanitized examples: your instruction, what the tool produced, how you verified it, and what you kept or changed. Do not include private chain of thought, credentials, full transcripts or proprietary account details.

## Most consequential AI-generated mistake
Describe the wrong assumption or implementation, how you discovered it and the regression guard you added.

## Productivity and limits
Separate observed time saved from subjective estimates. Explain when you stopped delegating and why.
